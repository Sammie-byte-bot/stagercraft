Our story

Great dancers were hard to find and harder to book

Nairobi has no shortage of talent. Crews rehearse in studios across the city, and solo dancers light up stages every weekend. But booking them meant chasing numbers, guessing at prices, and hoping the date held.

StageCraft puts it all in one place. Clients see who is free and what they cost. Dancers set their own prices and open dates. A 50% deposit locks the booking, so both sides can plan with confidence.

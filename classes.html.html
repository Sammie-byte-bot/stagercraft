The class experience

A good session has a rhythm

Classes are designed to move from preparation to practice, with clear instruction and time to make the choreography your own. The outline below is a sample format; each instructor may structure a class differently.

01

Warm up

Start with mobility, pulse-raising movement, and rhythm exercises to prepare your body safely for class.

02

Build technique

Break down foundational steps, timing, body coordination, and movement quality at a pace suited to the class level.

03

Learn choreography

Put the skills into a short sequence. Instructors may teach it in sections, then gradually connect the phrases.

04

Practice & cool down

Repeat, ask questions, and finish with a cool down. Filming or sharing class footage should always be agreed with the instructor.

Find your starting point

Choose a level that feels right

You do not need prior experience for beginner-friendly sessions. Level labels below are general guidance; check with the instructor if you are unsure which class suits you.

Start here

Beginner

For first-timers or dancers who want a refresher. Expect step-by-step breakdowns, repeated practice, and time to understand the basic rhythm and shapes.

Build confidence

Open level

For mixed experience levels. Instructors offer options or progressions so dancers can work at a comfortable challenge level.

Keep progressing

Intermediate

For dancers with some foundation in the style. Classes may move faster and focus more on detail, musicality, stamina, and performance quality.

Before you arrive

Come ready to move

For exact studio rules and clothing guidance, follow the instructor’s directions for the class you book. These are simple general suggestions.

01

Wear comfortable clothes

Choose clothes that let you move freely. Bring footwear appropriate to the style and studio floor; some sessions may be barefoot.

02

Bring water

Stay hydrated, arrive a little early, and let the instructor know about any movement limitations before class begins.

03

Ask before filming

Respect other students’ privacy. Ask the instructor and anyone visible in a recording before taking or sharing video.

Good to know

Class FAQs

Common questions to help you plan. Final booking, payment, cancellation, and studio policies should be confirmed with the actual class provider.

Can I join if I have never danced before?

Yes—look for a beginner class or ask the instructor whether the session is suitable for a first-timer. The sample listings on this page are examples, so confirm the real level before booking.

What should I wear or bring?

Wear comfortable clothing that allows movement and bring water. Footwear and any other requirements depend on the style and studio, so check the class-specific instructions.

How long is a class and what does it cost?

Durations and fees vary by provider. Any example times or fees shown on this page are placeholders and are not a live offer. Confirm the actual duration, price, and payment terms before you attend.

Do I need to book in advance?

Booking requirements and class capacity depend on the instructor. Contact the provider before travelling to confirm the schedule, space availability, and how to reserve a spot.

Can I book a private lesson or a group session?

Private and group options depend on instructor availability. Use the enquiry link to ask about your preferred style, group size, date, and location.
